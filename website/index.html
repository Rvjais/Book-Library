LibReady

A wooden-shelf library and reader for your PDF and EPUB books. Everything stays on your device — there is no server, no cloud sync, and nothing ever leaves your phone.

What it does

A shelf for every book you own

Import your PDFs and EPUBs, give each one a cover from its first page, and pick up reading right where you left off.

📚

Wooden shelf library

Book covers stand on dark-wood shelves with the date you last read them above each cover. Search, filter (All / Reading / Finished / Favourites), and sort your library however you like.

📖

Real page-turns

Choose from page curl, book flip, slide, fade, or instant turns. The current page curls over the next, so reading feels like a real book.

🎨

Four reader themes

Read in Paper, Sepia, Night, or Black. Adjust font size, line height, margins, justification, and serif/sans typography — all saved per book.

🔒

100% offline, always

No account, no server, no analytics, no ads, no network permission. Books, covers, bookmarks, and progress live only in your app's private storage.

🔄

Continue reading

A dashboard card shows your last book, progress, and a one-tap resume. Reading statistics and finished shelves track your journey.

➕

Import in batches

Add several books at once with a progress dialog. Duplicates are detected by file fingerprint, so the same book is never imported twice.

Privacy by design

Nothing leaves your device

LibReady does not collect any data. There is no analytics, no advertising, no cloud sync, and the release build declares no network permission on Android.

  • All books and covers are stored locally in the app's private Documents directory.
  • Bookmarks and progress live in a local database on your device.
  • No account required. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log into.
  • Deleting a book removes its file, cover, bookmarks, and reading progress.

Get started

From file to bookshelf in three steps

01

Import

Tap Add book and pick one or more PDF or EPUB files. LibReady copies them into its private storage.

02

Discover

Each book gets a cover from its first page, with the last-read date shown above it on your shelf.

03

Read

Tap a cover and turn pages with a real curl. Pick up exactly where you left off, anytime.
